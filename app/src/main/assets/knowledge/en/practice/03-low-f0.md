# Pitch Too Low: Raise the Speaking Position Without Strain

When average pitch is at or below 165 Hz, the feminization overall score is capped.

## Avoid these actions

- Do not speak while holding the larynx high.
- Do not press the larynx with your hand.
- Do not force pitch upward by shouting.
- Do not begin with falsetto.
- Do not tense the neck and jaw to maintain a higher pitch.

These methods may raise pitch briefly and make the voice tight, artificial, and effortful.

## Eight-minute practice

### 1. Record a baseline

Speak in your normal voice:

> Sunk cost is time, money, or emotion that has already been spent and cannot be recovered.

Use normal volume and a normal microphone distance.

### 2. Release the mouth and tongue

Gently move the jaw, tongue, and lips. Breathe in naturally through the nose without filling the lungs completely.

### 3. Make a small pitch glide

Use a comfortable `m` or `ng` and glide gently between adjacent notes.

Keep the range small. If the throat squeezes, pain appears, or the breath catches, reduce the range or stop.

### 4. Find a position that supports a full sentence

Move from the hum into:

> Rationally speaking, whether to continue or stop should depend only on future benefits and costs.

Try two or three slightly different starting pitches. Choose the version that can carry a full sentence comfortably, not the highest version.

### 5. Preserve language tones

Do not flatten every word into one pitch level. Languages naturally rise and fall. The goal is to change the overall speaking position, not fix every word at one pitch.

### 6. Transfer to a daily sentence

Use the new position for:

> Are you here? I’m waiting at the entrance.

Then answer one improvised question. The position is not learned until it survives a new sentence.

## What to check when listening back

Check clarity and effort before pitch. If pitch rises but you cannot finish a sentence, become increasingly tired, or naturalness drops, do not keep this version.

If naturalness and timbre score are both good and the result remains at 59, pitch is still the main problem.

If the result is only 20, pitch is not the only problem. Naturalness also needs work. Restore normal speech before working on pitch.

## When to stop

Stop immediately if you experience pain, burning, persistent hoarseness, frequent throat clearing, dizziness, or breathing discomfort.
