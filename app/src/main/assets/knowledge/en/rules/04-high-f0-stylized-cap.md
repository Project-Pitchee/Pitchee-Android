# High Pitch and Low Naturalness: “Eunuch Voice”

Average pitch is above 165 Hz.

Naturalness is below 50.

The feminization overall score is capped at 30.

This score corresponds to the perception users describe as a “eunuch voice”: the pitch is high, but the voice does not sound like normal speech.

## Score explanation

The pitch category passes.

The naturalness category does not.

The voice is both high and clearly removed from everyday speech, which tends to sound sharp, fake, squeezed, or suspended. The system therefore groups it as high pitch with low naturalness, does not treat it as feminine, and caps the result at 30.

The current timbre score is trained mainly on typical male and female voices and does not cover the “eunuch voice” combination. It is therefore not a valid basis for judging this branch of the rules. A high timbre score does not prove that this vocal approach is working.

## What to improve

Pitch is already high enough. The main weakness is naturalness. Pushing from 180 Hz to 200 Hz will not turn a eunuch voice into a normal female voice. It will only make the voice tighter and less natural. Restore normal connected speech first, then work on timbre and the bonus.
