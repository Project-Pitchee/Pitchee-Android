# High Pitch and Low Timbre Score: A “Bright and Thin” Male Voice

Average pitch is above 165 Hz.

Naturalness is at least 50.

Timbre score is below 50.

## Score explanation

The pitch category passes.

The normal-speech category passes.

The feminine timbre category does not pass.

The voice has become higher, but the overall timbre is not feminine. The high pitch remains, and the male voice remains as well. The overall score is therefore capped at 59.

The main problem is timbre, not pitch. Pushing pitch higher will not turn a bright, thin male voice into a female voice, and it may add vocal strain.

## What to improve

Raise the timbre score first, then reassess naturalness.
