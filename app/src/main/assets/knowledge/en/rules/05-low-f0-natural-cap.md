# Natural Voice and Pitch Too Low: “Cis Male Voice”

Naturalness is at least 50.

Average pitch is at or below 165 Hz.

The feminization overall score is capped at 59.

This result corresponds to a natural, stable male voice with low pitch, described here as a “cis male voice.”

## Score explanation

Naturalness has passed, which means the voice sounds close to normal speech.

Pitch has not crossed 165 Hz, so the feminine pitch category does not pass.

No matter how high the timbre score is, it cannot make up for pitch on its own. The overall score is therefore capped at 59.
