# Low Pitch and Unnatural: “Withdrawn and Low”

Average pitch is at or below 165 Hz.

Naturalness is below 50.

The feminization overall score is capped at 20.

This voice is low, tight, and held back. The corresponding perception is “withdrawn and low.”

## Score explanation

The pitch category fails.

The naturalness category fails.

No matter how high the timbre score is, it cannot offset low pitch and low naturalness. The final score is capped at 20.

When a voice stays pressed into a low position, it sounds withdrawn. When it also departs from everyday speech, it may sound tight, muffled, weak, or trapped in the same position from sentence to sentence. When pitch and natural expression both move away from the target, the voice sounds withdrawn.

## What to improve

Work on naturalness first. The voice should produce full sentences in a normal, connected way without pressing down or clamping at the start. After naturalness is stable, work on pitch separately. Changing both at once makes it easy to replace one unnatural pattern with another.
